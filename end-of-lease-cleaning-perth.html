Our guarantee

Bond-back guarantee: we re-clean until it passes

If your property manager or landlord lists a cleaning item on the exit inspection, we come back and re-clean it at no charge.

Guarantee terms

  1. Tell us within {{GUARANTEE_DAYS}} days of your clean and send the property manager’s written list of cleaning items.
  2. We return and re-clean the listed items free, at the first time the property manager allows access.
  3. The property must be vacant and unchanged since our clean, with power and water still connected.
  4. Covers items on our end of lease checklist plus any add-ons you booked. It excludes damage, wear and tear, permanent stains and areas we could not access on the day.
  5. The guarantee is a free re-clean. We do not pay out or refund bond money.
