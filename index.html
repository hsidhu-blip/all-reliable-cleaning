Our guarantee

Bond-back guarantee: we re-clean until it passes

If your property manager or landlord lists a cleaning item on the exit inspection, we come back and re-clean it at no charge.

Regular house and office cleans carry the same 3-day free re-clean guarantee.

Guarantee terms

  1. Tell us within 3 days of your clean and send the property manager’s written list of cleaning items.
  2. We return and re-clean the listed items free, at the first time the property manager allows access.
  3. The property must be vacant and unchanged since our clean, with power and water still connected.
  4. Covers items on our end of lease checklist plus any add-ons you booked. It excludes damage, wear and tear, permanent stains and areas we could not access on the day.
  5. The guarantee is a free re-clean. We do not pay out or refund bond money.

FAQ

Questions before you book

How much will my clean cost?

Every job gets a free quote. Starting prices are listed above. Your final price depends on size and condition, and we confirm it before we start.

Do you guarantee I get my bond back?

If your property manager flags a cleaning item within 3 days of our end of lease clean, we re-clean it free. We don’t pay out bond money.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave a key or lockbox code, or arrange access through your agent or building manager. We lock up when we finish.

Are pets OK?

Yes. Tell us about pets in the form. We remove pet hair, and we can add carpet steam cleaning or odour treatment.

Can I change or rebook my clean?

Yes. Tell us at least 24 hours before your booking and we move it at no cost.

How fast will you reply?

Quote requests received by 2pm, any day of the week, get a reply the same day. Call 0452 431 033 if it’s urgent.
