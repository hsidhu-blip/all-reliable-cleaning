FAQ

Office cleaning questions

How is office cleaning priced?

Small offices start from $99 per visit. Your price depends on floor area, amenities and how often we visit.

Do you require a contract or upfront bond?

No. There’s no upfront bond and no lock-in contract. Cancel any time with 48 hours’ notice.

How do your cleaners get in after hours?

We hold keys or access cards securely, use your alarm code and lock up after every visit. We follow your sign-in process.

We have an office dog. Is that a problem?

No. Tell us in the form and we’ll vacuum pet hair from floors and soft furnishings on every visit.

Can we change nights or pause over the holidays?

Yes. Tell us at least 48 hours before and we move, add or pause visits at no cost.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. We bring the vacuums, mops and products. If you prefer particular products, leave them out and we’ll use them.
