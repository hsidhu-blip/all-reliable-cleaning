Our guarantee

Bond-back guarantee: we re-clean until it passes

If your property manager or landlord lists a cleaning item on the exit inspection, we come back and re-clean it at no charge.

Guarantee terms

  1. Tell us within 3 days of your clean and send the property manager’s written list of cleaning items.
  2. We return and re-clean the listed items free, at the first time the property manager allows access.
  3. The property must be vacant and unchanged since our clean, with power and water still connected.
  4. Covers items on our end of lease checklist plus any add-ons you booked. It excludes damage, wear and tear, permanent stains and areas we could not access on the day.
  5. The guarantee is a free re-clean. We do not pay out or refund bond money.

FAQ

End of lease cleaning questions

How much does end of lease cleaning cost in Perth?

Prices start from $249 for a 1 bedroom unit. Your final price depends on bedrooms, bathrooms and condition, and we confirm it before we start.

What if my property manager isn’t happy?

Send us their list within 3 days and we re-clean the cleaning items free. That’s our bond-back guarantee.

Do I need to be there on the day?

No. Most renters leave a key, a lockbox code or arrange access through the agent. We lock up and confirm when we’re done.

I had pets. Can you remove the hair and smell?

Yes. We remove pet hair from floors, skirting boards and vents. Many leases require carpet steam cleaning after pets, so add it to your quote and we’ll do both in one visit.

My move date changed. Can I rebook?

Yes. Tell us at least 24 hours before your booking and we move it to a new date at no cost.

Is carpet steam cleaning included?

It’s an add-on, so you only pay for it if your lease requires it. Add it in the form and it goes on the same quote.
