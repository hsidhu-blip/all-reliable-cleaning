Who we are

All Reliable Cleaning provides end of lease, regular house, office and carpet steam cleaning in Perth, Western Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and how to contact us about it.

What we collect

  • Details you send us: your name, phone number, suburb, the service you need and any note you add when you request a quote.
  • How you found us: if you arrive from an online ad, the ad click reference and campaign details in the page address (for example Google Ads click IDs and UTM tags).
  • Website usage: pages viewed, buttons tapped (such as call buttons) and similar information collected with cookies by Google Analytics and Google Ads.

How we use it

  • To contact you with a quote, arrange and carry out your clean, and follow up afterwards.
  • To understand which ads and pages bring in enquiries, so we can improve our advertising.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we only use it for the purposes above.

Who we share it with

  • GoHighLevel, the system we use to manage enquiries and bookings.
  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads), to measure visits and ad results. When you request a quote, your phone number may be sent to Google in a hashed (scrambled) form to match the enquiry to an ad click. This is known as enhanced conversions.

These providers may store information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies from Google Analytics and Google Ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Blocking cookies won’t stop you from requesting a quote.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing {{EMAIL}} or calling 0452 431 033. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first and we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
