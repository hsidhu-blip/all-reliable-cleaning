What every carpet clean includes

Every room gets the same five-stage process, so carpets come up evenly with no patchy areas.

Before we clean

  • Pre-inspection for stains, wear and fibre type
  • Furniture moved where safe (beds and wardrobes stay)
  • Dry vacuum to lift loose grit

Pre-treatment

  • Traffic lanes pre-sprayed
  • Spot treatment for food, drink and mud stains
  • Pet accident areas treated

Hot water extraction

  • High-temperature rinse flushes dirt from the base of the fibres
  • Powerful extraction removes the water and soil
  • Edges and corners cleaned

Finish

  • Carpet groomed so fibres dry evenly
  • Deodoriser applied
  • Drying advice and a receipt for your records
